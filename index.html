Our story

Built on
relationships.

Groundrise was founded on a simple belief: the right introduction changes everything.

Ethan Davis
CEO & Founder

Ethan Davis started where most people overlook — in the details of a brand. Beginning in graphic design, he quickly realized the most powerful thing he could do wasn't create something beautiful. It was make someone else successful.

Over three years, Ethan embedded himself in media and content — building brands, growing audiences, and scaling creator careers from the ground up. What started small became an operation working with some of the top creators and clients in the world, spanning entertainment, business, and real estate.

The pivot to real estate wasn't a departure from media — it was the natural destination. Real estate professionals are among the most underserved when it comes to genuine brand strategy and social execution. Groundrise was built to close that gap.

Today, Ethan leads Groundrise with the same philosophy that started it all: stay humble, focus entirely on the client's success, and trust that the results speak for themselves. Serving agents and brokerages across the US.
